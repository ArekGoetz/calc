Math input & reading the plot

Use x, y, +, −, *, /, ^ and parentheses. Examples: 2*x-y, x^2+y^2, sin(x*y). Simple implicit multiplication works: 2x or x(y+1). Write x*y, not xy.

Functions: sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sinh, cosh, tanh, sqrt, abs, exp, ln, log, log10, floor, ceil, sign. Also min(a,b), max(a,b), atan2(y,x). Trig inputs are radians; ln and log are natural log. Constants: pi, e.

Arrows start at their grid points. The same distance represents one unit on both axes. In proportional mode, every vector is multiplied by the same factor, shown below the plot. The multiplier refits when the field, window, or density changes. Equal-length mode hides magnitude in arrow length. Color, when enabled, still shows relative magnitude. Arrows that extend past the viewing window are clipped at the border.

A small outlined circle marks a zero vector; a gray × marks an undefined or non-finite sampled vector. The plot samples the field, so singularities between samples may not be marked. Click or tap the graph to show a vector. It stays selected until you choose another point or clear it. Scroll over the graph to zoom about the pointer; the +, −, and Reset buttons also work on touchscreens. With the graph focused, use +/− to zoom, arrow keys to move the selected point, Home to center it, or Escape to clear it.
